Coronary Heart Disease

Coronary heart disease (CHD) develops gradually due to a combination of factors, including age, lifestyle behaviours, and clinical conditions such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, and smoking. Many individuals remain asymptomatic for years while cardiovascular risk continues to accumulate.

Early identification of elevated risk is therefore critical, as timely lifestyle changes and clinical interventions can substantially reduce the likelihood of adverse cardiac events. However, risk is often under-recognised in routine care until symptoms emerge or disease is advanced.

This report supports early risk awareness by analysing patient-level data to identify patterns, risk concentrations, and profiles associated with higher CHD prevalence, enabling more informed, prevention-focused decision making.

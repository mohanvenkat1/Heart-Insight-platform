📖 How to Publish & Get Your Tableau Public Link (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Open in Tableau
Open your workbook in Tableau Desktop or Tableau Public and connect to data/cleaned/heart_cleaned.csv.
Step 2: Save to Tableau Public
In the top menu, click Server → Tableau Public → Save to Tableau Public As... and sign in.
Step 3: Click the Share Icon
When the workbook opens in your browser on Tableau Public, click the Share button (3 connected dots) at the bottom right.
Step 4: Copy the Link
Copy the text in the Link box (e.g., https://public.tableau.com/views/...) and paste it into the URL bar above!

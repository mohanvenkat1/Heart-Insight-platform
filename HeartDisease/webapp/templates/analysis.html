💡 Key Insights

Actionable findings from our analysis

🚨 Gender Disparity

Males show a significantly higher prevalence of heart disease compared to females in the dataset, particularly in the 50-59 age group.

⚠️ Asymptomatic Risk

Patients with asymptomatic chest pain type have the highest correlation with heart disease, emphasizing the importance of routine screening.

✅ Exercise Matters

Patients without exercise-induced angina and higher maximum heart rates show lower disease prevalence, highlighting the protective role of cardiovascular fitness.

📊 Cholesterol Threshold

Cholesterol levels above 240 mg/dl correlate with significantly increased heart disease risk. Maintaining levels below 200 mg/dl is recommended.
